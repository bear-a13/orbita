2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Gravita is a personal to-do app in which each task is a planet. Higher priority means a bigger and heavier planet.
Problem: long to-do lists feel tiring and make every task look equally important.
Value: people see everything they need to do on one screen, recognize priorities by size, and interact with tasks physically by tilting, shaking, dragging and holding.
Audience: students and general users who want a simple, visual way to keep track of personal tasks.

3. HOW TO USE
No setup, login, credentials or sample files are needed. Two example planets appear on first launch. The interface is in Portuguese.
- Create: tap the yellow "+" button, choose Pequena (small), Média (medium) or Grande (large), type a title and tap the check button.
- Edit: tap a planet, change its data and tap the check button.
- Move: drag and release a planet, or tilt or shake the device.
- Complete: press and hold a planet for about 1.5 seconds, until the yellow ring closes. The planet then disappears.
Tilt and shake need motion sensors. With Reduce Motion on, tilt and shake effects are turned off; dragging, editing and completing still work.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None. The app uses no data providers, authentication, payment processors, AI services, analytics, ads or third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests. It uses Apple frameworks only (UIKit, UIKit Dynamics, Core Motion, SwiftData). Data is stored locally on the device.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same way in all regions. Its interface is in Portuguese (Brazil).
